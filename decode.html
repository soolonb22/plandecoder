Decode my NDIS plan

Upload your plan. We explain it in plain words: your budgets, what each line means, dates to watch, and questions to ask.

Your privacy
  • Your PDF is opened on your device. The file is not uploaded.
  • Only the plan's words are sent to our AI to explain them. We do not save them.
  • Your breakdown is kept on this device only. You can delete it any time.
  • Tip: you can cover your NDIS number before uploading. We don't need it.

Plan Decoder is not affiliated with the NDIA. This is a plain-language guide, not legal or financial advice. Always check your plan, the my NDIS app and ndis.gov.au before you spend. If something looks wrong, talk to your support coordinator, plan manager or the NDIA (1800 800 110).
